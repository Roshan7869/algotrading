# Telegram Reporting

Automated trade notifications and scheduled performance reports delivered via Telegram bot.

## Reports

- Daily performance summary
- Weekly strategy ranking
- Drawdown warnings
- Custom alert thresholds

## Trigger Phrases
- "send trade report", "alert on Telegram"
- "configure Telegram alerts", "daily summary"

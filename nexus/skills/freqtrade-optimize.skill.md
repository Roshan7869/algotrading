# Freqtrade Optimize

Optimization subsystem for strategy tuning: hyperparameter optimization, backtesting, and grid search.

## Capabilities
- Hyperopt: bayesian optimization of strategy parameters
- Backtesting: historical simulation with configurable date ranges
- Walk-forward analysis: time-series cross-validation
- WalkforwardOptimizer with robustness checks (WFR, Sharpe stability)
- Grid search over parameter spaces

## Trigger Phrases
- "optimize strategy parameters", "run hyperopt"
- "walkforward backtest", "tune indicators"
